Add PTZ camera by IP address (VISCA)…

Four numbers separated by dots, for example 192.168.1.100. Find it in the camera's on-screen menu or your router.

Add any IP camera by stream URL (NVR / Yi / phone / RTSP / MJPEG)…

Common URLs — Hikvision cam/NVR: rtsp://user:pass@ip:554/Streaming/Channels/101 (NVR channel 2 = 201…) · Dahua/Amcrest: rtsp://user:pass@ip:554/cam/realmonitor?channel=1&subtype=0 · Yi (RTSP firmware): rtsp://ip/ch0_0.h264 · Android IP Webcam: http://phone-ip:8080/video. These play in Live view and Multiview; PTZ control needs a VISCA camera. Cloud-only cameras (e.g. Amazon Blink) don't expose a local stream and can't be pulled in by any app.

Use an Android phone as a wireless camera…
  1. Install a camera-server app on the phone — IP Webcam (free) works great.
  2. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi network as this PC.
  3. In the app, tap Start server — it shows an address like http://192.168.1.23:8080.
  4. Click Scan network above — the phone appears under discovered devices; click Add. (Or add http://<phone-ip>:8080/video via the stream URL form.)

Button & axis mapping

Click Rebind then press the controller button (or move the axis) you want to use. Click Clear to unassign. Save a preset: just hold its button for a moment (tap recalls) — no shift needed, great for the adaptive joystick. You can also hold Preset shift + a preset, or arm Preset save mode and tap.

AI subject tracking

On the Cameras tab, click ◎ Track subject under the live view and drag a box around a person or object — the camera pans/tilts on its own to keep them centered. While the subject stands still the camera holds perfectly still, and it starts following the moment they actually move. The tracker runs fully on-device (no internet, no GPU). Moving the stick, pressing Esc, or STOP ALL instantly hands control back to you; a Stop AI tracking button can also be bound on the Controller tab.

Presets & saving

Save presets straight from the controller — no two-button chord. This is built for the Xbox Adaptive Controller / adaptive joystick, where holding a shift button and pressing a preset at the same time is hard. Tap a preset button to recall it; hold it to store the current position. Prefer a single press? Bind a Preset save mode button on the Controller tab to arm saving, then tap a preset.
